The Watchlist

Screen

Films and series — some watched, some loved, some regretted.
Entries marked my pick are things I found and added myself.
Click any row for a description, a verdict, and the conversation below.

Title Verdict
Mirzapur 2018– · Series · Crime / Thriller · Prime Video
Must Watch ★★★★★

Raw, violent, and unapologetically Indian. Mirzapur is one of those rare shows that feels genuinely alive — not polished to a global template. The world it builds in the Purvanchal belt is rich and unforgettable.

Kota Factory 2019– · Series · Drama · Netflix / TVF
Must Watch ★★★★★

Shot in black and white, it shouldn't work as well as it does. But it does. Jeetu Bhaiya is perhaps the most genuinely inspiring fictional mentor I've encountered — the show earns every tear.

Laal Singh Chaddha 2022 · Film · Drama · Netflix
Watch ★★★☆☆

A sincere but uneven adaptation. Aamir puts everything in, and some sequences are genuinely moving. But the film never quite escapes the shadow of the original, nor does it fully become its own thing.

Kill 2023 · Film · Action / Thriller · Netflix
Must Watch ★★★★☆

Claustrophobic, relentless, and completely unafraid of its own brutality. Kill earns its reputation. Indian action cinema found a new ceiling here. Not for the faint-hearted.

Game of Thrones 2011–2019 · Series · Fantasy / Drama · HBO / JioCinema
Must Watch ★★★★☆

Seasons 1–4 are nearly perfect television. The last two seasons are a cautionary tale about what happens when ambition meets deadline. Still essential. The fall just makes it more interesting to think about.

House of the Dragon 2022– · Series · Fantasy / Drama · HBO / JioCinema
Must Watch ★★★★☆

Slower, more politically intricate than its predecessor. The tragedy here is structural — you know roughly where it ends, which makes watching the characters walk toward it quietly devastating.

The Knight of Seven Kingdoms 2024– · Series · Fantasy · HBO / JioCinema
Depends ★★★☆☆

A quieter entry into the Westerosi universe. Dunk and Egg are charming companions. If you're already fatigued by the world-building, skip it. If you love the lore, it's a gentle pleasure.

Breaking Bad 2008–2013 · Series · Drama / Thriller · Netflix
Must Watch ★★★★★

Still the gold standard for character transformation on television. Every frame is intentional. The descent of Walter White is the most meticulously crafted tragedy I've watched. Required viewing.

Oppenheimer my pick 2023 · Film · Drama / Historical · Prime Video
Must Watch ★★★★★

Three hours that feel like a reckoning. Nolan finally found a subject worthy of his ambition. The physics, the politics, the moral collapse — all held together by Cillian Murphy's extraordinary stillness.

Chernobyl 2019 · Series · Drama / Historical · JioCinema
Must Watch ★★★★★

The cost of lies — institutional, political, human. Five episodes that function as both disaster drama and quiet philosophical horror. Legasov's monologue in the finale stays with you.
