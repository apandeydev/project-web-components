Gödel, Escher, Bach: An Eternal Golden Braid

Douglas R. Hofstadter

1979

A metaphorical fugue on minds and machines in the spirit of Lewis Carroll — weaving together Gödel's incompleteness theorems, Escher's visual paradoxes, and Bach's musical structures into a meditation on consciousness and self-reference.

Philosophy Consciousness Mathematics

What I Think

This book didn't just change how I think — it changed how I think about thinking. The strange loops Hofstadter constructs feel less like metaphors and more like mirrors. I keep returning to the Ant Fugue.
★★★★★

Being and Time

Martin Heidegger

1927

A foundational text of existential philosophy, asking what it means to exist in time — introducing concepts like Dasein, thrownness, and authenticity that permanently reshaped 20th-century thought.

Philosophy Existentialism Ontology

What I Think

Dense and demanding, but every ten pages there's a sentence that makes the difficulty worthwhile. The idea of being-toward-death doesn't terrify me as much as it clarifies. Still working through it.
★★★★★

Read

The Fabric of Reality

David Deutsch

1997

Deutsch argues that four fundamental strands — quantum physics, epistemology, evolution, and computation — together form a unified theory of everything. Bold, rigorous, and deeply optimistic about human knowledge.

Physics Epistemology Quantum

What I Think

Deutsch writes like someone who genuinely believes in the infinite reach of knowledge — and it's infectious. The many-worlds interpretation handled here with more clarity than anywhere else I've read.
★★★★★

The Brothers Karamazov

Fyodor Dostoevsky

1880

Dostoevsky's final novel — a philosophical and spiritual drama about faith, doubt, free will, and moral responsibility, centred around three brothers and a murdered father. Often called the greatest novel ever written.

Literature Philosophy Russian

What I Think

The Grand Inquisitor chapter alone is worth the whole read. Ivan's argument against God isn't answered — it's absorbed. Alyosha doesn't debate him; he kisses him. That said more than any rebuttal could.
★★★★★
